Delete your Palam account

For the Palam app by Palam · applies to your account and its data

You can delete your Palam account at any time, in the app or by email. Deletion is permanent — it cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Palam and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Email info@palamapp.com with the subject "Delete my account", sent from the email address your account uses — that is how we verify it is really you. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (your email address)
  • Your display name and profile, including your photo and anything you wrote
  • Your expert application, if you made one
  • Your bookings and session history

All of it is removed from our active systems within 30 days.

What can remain, and for how long

Backups: copies of your data in encrypted backups age out automatically within roughly 30 days after deletion.

Payment records: Stripe, our payment processor, keeps transaction records for as long as financial law requires. Those records live with Stripe — card details never touch Palam's servers.

Questions

Not sure about something before you delete? Write to info@palamapp.com — a human reads it.
