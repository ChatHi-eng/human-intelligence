Cancellations & no-shows

Plans change. Here is exactly what happens when they do. Times are measured from the session's scheduled start, and a session counts as a no-show when someone hasn't joined within 10 minutes of it.

What happened What we do
Either side cancels or reschedules more than 24 hours before the start Free. The customer is refunded in full.
The customer cancels less than 24 hours before the start The payment is not refunded; the expert keeps the payout.
The expert cancels, at any time The customer gets a full refund, always.
The customer doesn't show up The expert keeps the full payout; no refund.
The expert doesn't show up The customer gets a full refund, and the expert gets a strike. Two strikes within 90 days and the expert is removed from Palam.

Either participant can report a no-show from the booking screen, from 10 minutes after the scheduled start until 72 hours after it. A human reviews every report within 48 hours. If you dispute how a session went, email info@palamapp.com within 48 hours of the session and we will adjudicate.
